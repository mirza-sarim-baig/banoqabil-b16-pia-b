American Brahman
The American Brahman is a breed of zebu cattle (Bos indicus) that was developed in the United States from cattle breeds imported from India. Brahman cattle are known for their ability to withstand high temperatures and humidity, as well as their resistance to parasites and diseases. They are often used in crossbreeding programs to improve the hardiness and productivity of other cattle breeds.
